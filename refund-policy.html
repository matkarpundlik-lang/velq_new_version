Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Contact: velqstudio@gmail.com

1. Before work begins

Contact VELQ in writing if you wish to cancel. Any refund will be determined according to the agreed project terms, work already performed, committed costs and applicable law.

2. After work begins

Where a project is cancelled after work has commenced, we will review the work completed and any approved non-recoverable costs. The signed agreement governs the amount due or refundable.

3. Third-party costs

Domain, hosting and other third-party purchases may be subject to the provider’s own cancellation and refund rules. These costs will be considered in accordance with the project agreement and applicable law.

4. Requesting a refund

Email us with your project reference and reason for cancellation. We will review the request and communicate the outcome and any applicable refund method and timeline.

5. Your rights

Nothing in this policy limits mandatory rights or remedies under applicable law. No blanket non-refundable deposit rule is imposed by this page.
