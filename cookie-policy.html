Cookie Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains how VELQ uses cookies and similar browser storage on this website.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. Similar technologies, including local storage, can remember settings and help a website function.

2. Categories

CategoryPurposeChoice
Strictly necessaryCore website operation, security and remembering your consent choice.Always active
AnalyticsWould help us understand website usage and improve the experience, if an analytics provider is added.Optional
AdvertisingWould support advertising measurement or personalised advertising, if an advertising provider is added.Optional
Current setup: The website's consent interface supports optional analytics and advertising categories. Those categories should remain inactive unless a provider is installed and you choose to allow them.

3. Managing your preferences

When the cookie notice appears, you can accept all optional categories, reject optional categories, or open preferences to make a choice. You can revisit your choice using the cookie preferences control on the website, where available. Clearing browser storage may reset your saved choice.

4. Browser controls

You can also use your browser settings to block or delete cookies. Blocking strictly necessary storage may affect some website features or prevent your preferences from being remembered.

5. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when our website technologies change. Questions can be sent to velqstudio@gmail.com.
